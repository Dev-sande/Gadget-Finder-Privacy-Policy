Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

Summary

Gadget Finder works entirely on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send any of your data to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores

When you save a gadget, the app keeps the name you give it, its type, its Bluetooth identifier, and when it was last seen and how strong its signal was. This information is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded or shared.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth to listen for signals from nearby gadgets so it can show how close they are. It only listens; it does not connect to, control or read data from your gadgets. Signals from other people's devices may appear briefly in the "Nearby" list while you scan, but they are kept in memory only and are discarded when you leave that screen unless you choose to save one.

Location

On Android 12 and newer, the app asks only for "Nearby devices" permission and never for location. On Android 11 and older, Android requires location permission for any Bluetooth scan. The app asks for it for that reason only, and never reads your GPS position or tracks where you are.

Vibration

The app vibrates your phone while tracking to signal how close a gadget is. This needs no personal data.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent or share any data, and the app contains no third-party advertising or tracking code.

Deleting your data

Remove a gadget by long-pressing it on the home screen and choosing "Remove gadget". Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the app and on this page with a new effective date.
